>_ duckware

Free apps for writing, PDFs, notes and spreadsheets.

They open and save the files you already use (Word, PDF, Excel), keep everything on your own computer, and cost nothing. No accounts, no cloud, no subscriptions.

Download the launcher One icon for your taskbar: it installs and updates the apps you pick.

Windows may say Windows protected your PC, because the installers aren't code-signed (that costs money every year). Click More info, then Run anyway. After that the apps keep themselves up to date.
