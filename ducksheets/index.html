DuckSheets

A spreadsheet that opens and saves Excel workbooks (.xlsx, .xlsm) and CSV files, with about 110 of Excel's functions, formatting, sorting, filters, frozen panes and charts. Anything it doesn't edit yet stays in your Excel file unchanged.

Download DuckSheets-Setup.exe What's new

Windows may say Windows protected your PC, because the installers aren't code-signed (that costs money every year). Click More info, then Run anyway. After that the apps keep themselves up to date.

// what it does

Excel files stay Excel files

Formulas, formats, widths, merged cells, frozen panes, filters and charts are kept as Excel keeps them.

Formulas

SUM, IF, XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, TEXT, dates, statistics, PMT and about 100 more, with , or ; between arguments.

Typing like Excel

Select a cell and type. 10% becomes a percentage, 20.10.2026 a date; F4 switches $.

Fill handle

Drag to copy formulas and continue series: numbers, weeks, months, weekdays, dates.

Data tools

AutoSum, sort, filter with search, freeze panes, find and replace.

Charts

Column, bar, line and pie, saved as real Excel charts.
